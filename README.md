One image summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026: a
magnitude 6.8 earthquake in central Peru, a Russian missile reported down
in Poland during overnight strikes on Ukraine, record foreign inflows into
Indian shares, and KL Rahul's Test century in Wellington. The footer adds
six more items from the day: the UK expelling Myanmar's diplomat, the
Denmark–US–Greenland pact, Mamdani's 2032 Rikers deadline, Nepal's PM
heading to Delhi, US sanctions on four Iranians, and Trump's Iran remarks.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory. Run it with
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`.
